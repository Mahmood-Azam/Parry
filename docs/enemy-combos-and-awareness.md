# Combat readability and enemy territories

- Player status panel: labeled HP (244 × 8), shared fire/ultimate meter (244 × 10), ultimate/charged-slash progress, Shadow cooldown/recharge and guard status. Enemy HP stays 48 × 4, with a short amber damage trail. Boss HP stays 280 × 6. World bars hide behind the camera and beyond 22–25 m. Unrelated debug readouts and combat pop-up text remain suppressed.
- Normal skeletons choose a single hit or a three-hit combo per attack opportunity (45% triple by default, configurable with `triple_attack_chance`). Three tiny amber ticks beneath their HP bar telegraph only triple attacks from the first windup; successful parries brighten the ticks. First animation lasts 0.88 s; following swings last 0.64 s; recovery tails are cut at 78%. Damage and parry contact still use the visible animation's normalized contact, not separate timers. Replaying a strike explicitly rewinds the clip.
- A fresh RMB press or F parries each strike. Holding RMB becomes ordinary blocking; it does not automatically parry the chain. Each success fills a small gold pip below the skeleton's health bar. Deflect all three strikes in the same combo, against the same defender, to ignite and immediately kill the skeleton. A block, miss, dodge, interruption or new combo cannot contribute to that execution. Early deflections consume that swing without the old full-duration stun cancelling the remaining strikes.
- Single-strike parries, bosses and gremlins retain ordinary parry stun; they cannot trigger the triple-only execution. Each parry adds fire once to the shared 0–200 combat/ultimate reserve. Its single track fills orange then violet over the orange. Attacks, charged slashes, decay and ultimates all spend this same reserve. See `ultimates.md`.
- Successful player parries alternate a 0.32 s upper-body sword deflection. Both arms follow the fitted right-hand hilt; the weapon socket is never offset. Perfect parries get a slightly stronger motion. The pose blends back out and is interrupted by dash, attacking, damage stun or death.
- Normal skeletons use a 0.90 s reset after finishing a single/triple attack, giving breathing room without the original 2.15 s pause. Strike speed, windup and random pattern selection remain intact; a real stun still grants its full punish window without adding another long recovery afterward. Boss cooldowns are unchanged; mixed-wave gremlins use a separate 1.45 s cooldown.
- Skeletons notice targets within 10 m; mages within 11 m. Acquisition requires line of sight. All use a spawn-anchored 16 m leash, including the Mutant (initial sight radius 9 m). Targets more than 2.5 m above/below are not chased between floors. Returning enemies ignore targets until home, then reacquire normally; health is not reset. Committed melee strikes finish before returning.
- Melee contact cannot pass through world walls. Nearby enemies separate softly during approach and slide along encountered walls. This is local steering, not a baked multi-floor navigation mesh.
- Portal arrivals re-anchor territory to their final courtyard destination, not their temporary rooftop position. Player starts at (35, 0.15, -64), over 13 m from the first enemies.
- The first New Game reuses the already-built campus instead of reloading it. Cinematic resources load on a background resource thread while the title is visible; a loading message renders before main-thread construction. A subsequent New Game still reloads to guarantee a complete progression reset.

Validation scripts: `tests/enemy_combos.gd`, `tests/new_game_start.gd`; regression coverage includes combat impacts, portal arrivals, mage/Mutant behavior and Options.
